TECH OLIGARCHS
Build the empire. Become the monopoly.

Age of Empires III, reskinned for the AI race. Your Founder is your Explorer hero: scouts, fights, gathers and builds, and revives at HQ. Engineers gather Data, Power, Capital and Chips. Earn XP to call Investor Shipments from your Home City, capture Fiber Nodes on the central data pipeline for trickle income, and late-game Gigafactories auto-produce for you. Out-age or out-gun the rival founder at NEXA Corp.

How you win

  • Monopoly: Age up Garage → Seed → Scale-up → IPO → Monopoly first, like AoE3's five ages.
  • Domination: Destroy the NEXA Founder HQ (top right, red).
  • You lose if your HQ falls, or NEXA hits Monopoly first.

First 60 seconds

  • Tap an Engineer → tap a blue Data node, yellow Power node, green Capital node or purple Chip node.
  • Train more Engineers at your HQ.
  • Build a Hacker House, then an AI Lab, then bots.
  • Select bots → tap an enemy or use Attack Rival HQ.
  • Send any unit to a glowing Fiber Node on the middle pipeline and hold it to capture trickle income.
  • Spend each Investor Shipment the moment it lands.

Difficulty

Whole map on one screen, no scrolling in battle. Works with mouse or touch. No save, one sitting, about 8 to 15 minutes per match.

How to Play

Economy

  • Data ◆ from user clusters: trains Engineers, feeds age-ups.
  • Power ⚡ from solar fields: buildings.
  • Capital $ from investor towers: bots, labs, age-ups.
  • Chips ▣ from silicon seams: turrets, fabs, AGI bots.
  • Engineers carry 10 per trip back to HQ.
  • Power Plant: +0.7 Power/s. Data Center: auto +0.7 Data/s, trains AGI. Gigafactory (IPO): auto +1.0 Chips/s and +0.6 Capital/s.
  • Fiber Nodes: 3 sites on the central pipeline. Stand a unit on one for 4s to capture it: +0.5 Capital/s and +0.3 Data/s each, forever, until NEXA takes it back.
  • Investor Shipments: gathering, building, training and kills earn XP. Each XP level = 1 shipment card at HQ: Capital, Data, Chips, Power, 2 Engineers, or a bot squad.

War & Ages

  • Founder (you): your Explorer hero. Ranged, tough, gathers double, builds, and revives at HQ 20s after falling. Never leaves the game for good.
  • AI Agent: cheap melee bot, Garage.
  • Security Bot: ranged bot, Seed.
  • AGI Bot: elite, Scale-up, from Data Center.
  • Firewall Turret: auto-shoots enemies in range.
  • Age up at the HQ. Each age buffs gather speed and unlocks tech.
  • NEXA sends raids that grow over time. Defend your HQ.

Controls: tap/click to select. With an Engineer selected, tap a node to gather. With an army selected, tap ground to move or an enemy to focus fire. Build menu places buildings. Space pauses.
